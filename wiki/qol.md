# Quality of life

Click the minimap icon or type `/everlook` to open the Everlook page, and again to close it. Settings > AddOns > Everlook reaches the same page. Those are the only two entry points, and `/everlook` takes no arguments. The radial menu key and the smart island key are other ways to use the addon, but they do not open the page. One native Blizzard Settings page holds the signing status and the Eager scan button, which runs one scan now. Its subpages in the left list are Quests, Vendors, Loot and mail, Social, Chat, Tooltips, Map, Interface, Smart island, and Radial menu, each grouping its modules under section headings, and Collected data, which holds the data browser. Every module starts disabled. Its options become available when enabled. Preferences persist in `EverlookDB.qol`; they are separate from the gathered world data. Each module is its own addon under Everlook in the addon list, so a player can also turn one off there to keep its code from loading. Its page rows then go away and its saved preferences stay.

## Wiring

`Everlook/module.lua` owns module registration, default values, saved options, and event dispatch. A module addon registers with `Everlook.module.register` and names its addon, its settings page and its place on that page. Defaults and the first apply wait for that addon's own `ADDON_LOADED`. Enabled modules receive only their registered events. Native Settings proxies read and write through `Everlook.module.get` and `Everlook.module.set`. `Everlook.module.enabled` is false for a module whose addon is not loaded, so one module can ask about another.

An extension adds options, settings rows and events to a module that another addon registered, through `Everlook.module.extend`. Its options are saved under the host. Its events arrive only while the host is enabled, and its `apply` runs after the host's. An extension section with the host's section name joins it, after the host's rows or in front of the host key it names in `before`. A host calls hooks of its own through `Everlook.module.extensions`. The Smart island's feeds, warnings, experience detail and Alt-right-click sharing are extensions in `Everlook_Island<Name>` addons. The island calls their `refresh` after each quest refresh and their `follow` with each fresh vitals snapshot, and they share helpers from `Everlook.island_feed_kit`.

`Everlook/settings.lua` builds the page at login, after every module addon has registered and its key bindings exist. `Settings.RegisterVerticalLayoutCategory` and `Settings.RegisterAddOnCategory` register one Everlook page. A vertical subcategory holds each subject in the page list in `module.lua`, and a canvas subcategory holds the data browser frame. Blizzard section-header, checkbox, slider, and button initializers build their contents. Permanent hooks check their module toggle on each callback; disabling a module stops its timers and restores changes where applicable.

`tests/module.lua` covers the registry on its own. `tests/qol.lua` exercises module behavior through isolated client mocks. The addon test runner includes it. In-game verification still needs the actual client's frames, merchant responses, and flight paths.
